What you'll need

  1. 01 Ask for your itemized bill

    Contact the billing office of your hospital or doctor and ask for an itemized bill that lists every charge with its code. Under federal law (HIPAA), you have the right to a copy of your billing records, and they generally must respond within 30 days. Your rights (HHS)

  2. 02 Get it as a PDF

    Ask them to email it or post it to your patient portal. It usually comes as a PDF. Save the PDF to your phone or computer.

  3. 03 Upload it below

    One bill at a time, up to 5 MB. We read every charge and you check it before anything else happens.
