01

The short version

  • Candorify points out possible issues on your medical bill. These are suggestions to check, not findings.
  • Candorify is not 100% accurate. It can miss real mistakes and it can flag charges that are correct.
  • We never say a charge is definitely wrong. Only your provider or insurer can confirm that.
  • You decide what to ask about, and you send any letter yourself.
  • We remove your personal details before anything reads your bill, delete your bill when you finish, and never sell your data.

02

Suggestions, not verdicts

Every item Candorify shows you is a possible discrepancy to review. Words like "possible duplicate" or "math doesn't add up" mean "worth asking about." They never mean the charge is confirmed to be wrong.

Candorify does not:

  • verify charges or confirm that an error exists
  • guarantee that you will save money or get a refund
  • replace a billing auditor, patient advocate, doctor, lawyer, or financial advisor
  • give medical, legal, or financial advice

Nothing is settled until your provider or insurance company reviews it and agrees.

03

Accuracy

Candorify combines automatic checks with AI. Both can be wrong. In particular:

  • Reading a PDF is not perfect. We may misread a code, a price, or a quantity, so please check the charges screen against your bill.
  • We may miss real mistakes, and we may flag charges that are correct.
  • Plain-language explanations of billing codes and translations into other languages may be incomplete or imprecise.
  • The accuracy figures we publish come from testing on made-up sample bills. Results on real bills may differ.

04

Your decisions

  • You choose which items to include in a letter, and you can mark any item as fine.
  • Letters are drafts. Read and edit them before sending. They are sent from your own email, not by Candorify.
  • Don't skip or delay a payment only because Candorify flagged something. Ask your provider first, and keep an eye on any payment deadlines.
  • Acting on any suggestion is your own choice and responsibility.

05

Your bill and personal details

  • Your PDF is read on our server and deleted right away. We keep only the list of charges (codes, descriptions, quantities and prices), the total, the date of service and the account number printed on the bill. We don't keep your name or address.
  • Reading a bill is automatic and not perfect. Unusual layouts can be misread, so check the charges we show you.
  • Your bill itself is never sent to an AI. Only text that needs translating goes to our AI provider, Google (Gemini): the follow-up questions, plain-language explanations of billing codes, and a copy of your letter with your name, address and account number hidden first.
  • Your original PDF is never changed.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.

06

How long we keep it

  • Your bill and its details are permanently deleted when you finish your review.
  • If you don't finish, they are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • We keep only anonymous answers from the short survey and the optional follow-up: how many suggestions you found accurate, whether you contacted your provider, how much you saved, and how satisfied you were. They are not linked to your account or your bill, and they make up the numbers on our Our results page.
  • You can delete your account and everything linked to it at any time from Your account.

07

Your account

  • You need an account to use Candorify, and you must be 18 or older.
  • Keep your password private. "Remember me" keeps you signed in on that device, so only use it on a device you trust.
  • Only upload bills that are yours, or that you have permission to review.

08

Payments

Paid plans are handled by Stripe. You enter your card on Stripe's secure page, and Candorify never sees or stores your card number.

09

Limits of responsibility

  • Candorify is provided as is, without any promise that it is complete, accurate or error-free.
  • Because you upload your own bill, Candorify is not a HIPAA-covered entity.
  • To the extent the law allows, Candorify is not responsible for decisions you make based on its suggestions, for amounts you pay or don't pay, or for disclosure of information in your bill.

10

Changes and questions

Candorify is a student project at NJIT and is still a prototype. These terms have not yet been reviewed by a lawyer and may change. When they do, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
